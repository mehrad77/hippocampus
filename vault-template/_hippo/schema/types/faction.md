Organizations: institutions, companies, offices, groups of people.

## Is
- A university, or one of its departments
- A government agency, an embassy, a bank
- A landlord's agency, an employer, a club

## Is not
- One person who works there (that's a `character`)
- The building it's in (that's a `location`)
