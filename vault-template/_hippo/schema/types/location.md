Places: cities, neighbourhoods, offices, homes, campuses.

## Is
- A city or a district
- An apartment that's being considered or rented
- The office where an appointment happens

## Is not
- The organization that runs it (that's a `faction`)
- A website or portal (that's `lore` or an `item`)
