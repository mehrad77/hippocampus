A circle of particular people: a team, a class, a family chat, a club. People join with a `member_of` relation, or by naming the group in their tags; its guidelines and cadence then apply to them too, after their own and their archetype's.

## Is
- The Harbor University study group
- The flatmates in Lisbon
- The family group chat

## Is not
- A kind of person (that's an `archetype`)
- An institution or company (that's a `faction`)
